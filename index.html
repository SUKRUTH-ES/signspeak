    How it works

    1. Hand tracking: MediaPipe Hand Landmarker finds 21 3D keypoints per hand, for up to two hands, every frame.
    2. Features: keypoints are re-centred on the wrist and scaled, so position and distance from the camera don't matter.
    3. Classifier: a k-nearest-neighbour model matches the live pose against the pretrained ASL alphabet and your own recorded signs.
    4. Output: stable signs are committed. Letters join into words, and everything is spoken with the Web Speech API.

    🔒 Video never leaves your device. All inference runs locally.
